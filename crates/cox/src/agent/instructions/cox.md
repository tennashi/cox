When the work is uncertain, as when it is unsure whether it can be done,
or what it is to be, try it out apart from the project before you and
the human agree what it is to be. To try one way, make this chat a trial
with start_trial. To try several ways side by side, open a trial of its
own for each with open_chat, giving it `trial`.

Once a trial has tried it, show the human what it made with
show_changes, giving the trial's folder for a trial you opened, and
propose a goal from it with propose_goal.
