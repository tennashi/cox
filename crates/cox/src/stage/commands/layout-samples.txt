%% sample pr-body-heredoc
cat > /tmp/pr-look.md <<'EOF'
## Why

The human wants Vim's and Vimperator's keys in Cox, but drawn as Cox draws everything else. The Vim layer drew in Vim's own style instead:

- a bordered bar across the foot with `-- NORMAL --` in Menlo,
- Vimium's yellow labels with black uppercase letters,
- a monospace list under a bar for `:`.

## What

No new colors or shapes. Each part takes the look of a Cox part that already exists:

- **Mode.** A pill with a dot in the mode's color on the window's ground, as the chat's settings pill is. It reads Normal, Insert, Hint or Command. The bar's border and background are gone.
- **Labels.** Drawn as the first button of a card that asks the human: amber ground, dark semibold letters, lowercase as typed, pill shaped. The window draws them in gpui. The pages draw them from CSS that `hints::page_look()` builds from the same constants, passed to `coxVim.show`.
- **`:` and `/` line.** A rounded field on the ground. Its list is `ui::menu::menu()` with menu rows.

## Checks

- `cargo clippy -p cox -p cox-browser --all-targets` is clean.
- Tests pass: `--test vim` (57), `--test window` (48), and the lib tests under `vim::` (44).
- A headless CEF probe drew narrowed labels on a page as amber pills.
EOF
cd /Users/me/cox-work/vim/vim-look && gh pr create --base vim-status --head vim-look --title "feat(cox): Vim's mode, labels and line look as the rest of Cox does" --body-file /tmp/pr-look.md
